# imtihan_app

Imtihan is Pakistan's AI-powered exam preparation app. Upload any textbook chapter and get 20 board-exam MCQs instantly. Practice with a complete past paper bank covering all Pakistan boards — Punjab, Sindh, KPK, Federal, and AKU — across every grade and subject. Get instant explanations from your AI tutor, track your weak topics, and prepare for MDCAT, ECAT, CSS, and all competitive exams. Study smarter. Score higher.
